Part 2: The Three MRX Vulnerability Checks
Run every file through this filter before it touches a standard AI window.
1
The PII Sanity Test
The Question: Does this document contain specific client names, proprietary product concepts under active NDAs, or identifiable participant data?
The Rule: If yes, it never touches a free consumer AI interface. First strip the PII, or confirm you are operating in an enterprise environment with zero data-training defaults.
2
The Context-to-Token Ratio
The Question: Are you feeding Claude an entire 300-page data dump in one prompt, or processing it in tactical batches?
The Rule: A massive context window is not an invitation to abuse it. Teach your team to run structured, small-batch sample validations before processing full client datasets.
3
Chat vs. Tool-Building
The Question: Are your analysts re-typing the same prompt instructions into a fresh chat window every morning?
The Rule: If you are repeating a prompt more than three times, stop using standard chat. Turn that instruction set into a permanent workspace asset — a Claude Project or custom operational script — so the tool learns your agency's exact reporting structure.
What to Avoid Automating on Day 1
Many agencies fail because they automate the hardest tasks first.
Do Not Hand Over
  • Final methodology design for complex tracking studies
  • Deep stakeholder interpretation — reading between the lines of what a client said vs. what they actually meant
Start Here Instead
  • Formatting messy cross-tabs
  • Drafting initial survey response codes
  • Running first-pass sentiment tags across thousands of open-ended text answers
